# EMBERROOT — the setting

## The world

The world is the inside of a tree. Not a metaphor: the **Emberroot** is a dead
colossus whose root galleries run for hundreds of miles under everything, and the
holds are stone platforms fused into those galleries, hung over drops nobody has
ever reached the bottom of.

The tree died a long time ago. The fire in it did not. **Ember** runs through
inlaid channels cut into every floor, wall and pillar — the light people read by,
cook by, and navigate by. It is going out. Slowly, unevenly, and without anyone
being able to say why.

## Ember, rites and shards

**Ember** is heat, light, and the medium every rite borrows from.

**Rites** are inscribed patterns that pull ember through a person to do something
with it. They cost EP because the ember has to come from somewhere, and the
somewhere is you. Every rite carries an **affinity**.

**Shards** are ember that crystallised instead of burning. They are currency,
crafting stock, and the reason anybody goes down into the galleries at all. Ten
kinds, from common cinder to a crown, which most people will never see.

## The four affinities

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **EMBER** | fire, speed, the borrowed kind of power |
| **ROOT** | weight, endurance, things that hold |
| **BONE** | mask-work, warding, the rites the Quiet still perform |
| **HOLLOW** | absence, drain, taking ember back out of a thing |

They oppose in two pairs — **EMBER ↔ HOLLOW** and **ROOT ↔ BONE**. Striking a
target with the affinity that opposes it **resonates**, for close to double
damage. Striking it with its own affinity is **discordant**, for about half.

There is no third state and no resistance table to memorise. The whole tactical
game is knowing what a thing is and sending the right person at it — which is
also why the party is four people and the line is three.

## The Quiet

The masked things in the galleries are not monsters and were never evil. They are
the tree's caretakers: wardens, crawlers, choirs, cut and set to a job. The job
outlasted the reason for it by an age. They are still reading the writ, still
holding the note, still sealing what was sealed.

A Gallery Warden will read its writ before it acts. It always reads the writ.

## The route

Holds are joined by walked roads, recorded on the route map: **The Sill**,
**Lantern Hold**, **Low Crossing**, **The Standing One**, **Quiet Stair**,
**Rootplaza**, **Hollow Reach**, and **Crownfall**.

The party is at Rootplaza. The inlay past the Quiet Stair has gone cold, which it
has never done, and the Warden who went down to read the writ on it has not rung
the bell since.
