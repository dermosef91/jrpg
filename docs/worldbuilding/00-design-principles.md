# Design Principles

The constraints that generated this setting. When a new idea is proposed, check it
against this list first — most rejected ideas fail here rather than on quality.

## Hard exclusions

These are not "use sparingly." They are out.

- **No chosen one.** No prophecy, no bloodline destiny, no "only you can." The party
  are licensed professionals doing their actual job, and the job escalates.
- **No macguffin.** Nothing is collected. No crystals, shards, orbs, keys, or
  fragments-of-a-whole. What escalates is *information*, not inventory.
- **No final boss.** The last act has no combat climax. The climax is a disclosure
  and a vote.
- **No Dark Lord, no evil empire, no scrappy rebels.** Every faction is a coherent
  survival ethic held by people acting in good faith under scarcity.
- **No elemental wheel.** No fire/ice/lightning, no rock-paper-scissors typing.
- **No stock classes.** No warrior/mage/healer/thief. Party roles are real
  professions in this economy.
- **No silent or amnesiac protagonist.** Everyone has opinions and remembers things.
- **No power-of-friendship resolution.** The party's internal disagreements are the
  factional conflict in miniature and do not get resolved by sentiment.

## Positive commitments

- **The antagonist force is a natural fact, not an intention.** The Ember is a
  variable star in a dim phase. Nobody caused it. Nobody can fix it. The entire game
  is about adapting to something indifferent.
- **The most dangerous character is never fought and never lies.** See Verity
  Anneal-of-the-Ninth (`04-story.md`).
- **Every reveal recontextualizes, never merely adds.** Each act should retroactively
  change the meaning of the previous act rather than extending a list of facts.
- **The setting's magic system is its theme.** Grafting and ring-reading are about
  legacy, memory, and the editability of the past. Combat should feel like the same
  verb as investigation.
- **The map is complicit.** The cover-up survived eleven generations because of
  geography and social snobbery, not because of a conspiracy's competence.
- **Refuse to grade the player.** The endgame branches are not ranked. No ending is
  the "true" one, and the game never tells you which was right.

## Tonal target

Procedural and lived-in, not epic. The opening hours should read as competent people
doing careful, respected, boring work — an audit — in a world that is quietly ending
on a published schedule. The horror is actuarial.
