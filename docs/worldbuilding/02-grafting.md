# Grafting, Ring-Reading, and Cant

There is no elemental magic. There are three related disciplines, all of which are
licensed trades with guilds, malpractice standards, and paperwork.

## Grafting

The manipulation of growth: accelerating or arresting plant tissue, splicing living
matter to living matter, and redirecting an organism's own healing responses against
itself or in its favor.

Grafting is slow by nature. A graft is *placed*, then **matures** over a period, then
takes effect. This is true at every scale — a battlefield graft matures over turns, a
Strider drive-graft matures over a season. Nothing in this discipline is instantaneous,
which is the single most important texture in the setting: **you commit before you
know.**

A placed graft can be:

- **Matured** — left alone to reach full effect.
- **Harvested early** — cashed out for a reduced, immediate effect.
- **Excised** — cut out by an opponent before it matures, wasting the investment.

## Ring-reading

A Strider's growth rings are not merely age markers. They are a **memory medium**:
traumatic seasons, migrations, plagues, famines, and political decisions are encoded
in the wood chemistry, laid down annually and legible to a trained reader.

Ring-reading is a respected, boring, bureaucratic profession. **Audit circuits** travel
between Boles verifying official histories against what the wood actually remembers —
mediating migration-rights disputes, diagnosing sick Striders, and certifying council
claims. The player party is one of these circuits.

### The forensic principle

Natural ring chemistry is **noisy**. Real years vary — rainfall, disease, insect load,
light. The signature of a *falsified* record is not contradiction but
**anomalously low variance**: a written passage is too smooth, too consistent, too
well-behaved across a span where nature would have wobbled.

This is the core investigative verb of the game and the core of the ring-reading
minigame: you are not looking for a lie, you are looking for a stretch of history that
is suspiciously well-behaved.

### Writing

Ring-memory can be **written**, not just read. This is the dangerous half of the
discipline, and it is restricted to cant-lineages.

## Cant

**Cant-singing** is matrilineal call-and-response communication with the Strider
itself — the practical interface for directing a living city: requesting growth,
diagnosing distress, negotiating a migration heading. Cant-singers are infrastructure,
not entertainers.

Cant-lineages are also the only practitioners capable of **writing** to ring-memory.
Technique is inherited and hand-specific: a trained reader can identify *whose lineage*
made an edit the way a document examiner identifies handwriting, even centuries later.

Ring-record physically degrades and must be periodically **re-inked** to persist.
Cant-singers inherit maintenance duties over specific passages, taught as girls,
performed for life. Most never learn why a given passage matters — only that it must
hold.

## Party roles

Roles come from the professions above, not from RPG archetypes:

| Role | Discipline | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Cant-singer** | Cant | Reads targets, writes and rewrites grafts, interfaces with the Strider. Support that is structurally necessary rather than decorative. |
| **Graft-medic** | Grafting | Tissue-splicing healer. Places restorative grafts and excises hostile ones. |
| **Coring** | Heartwood quarrying | Structural specialist. Navigates by trace-dating; absorbs and redirects force. |
| **Windreader** | Forecasting | Storm and light prediction. Battlefield control and information. |
