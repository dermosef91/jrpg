# Cast

## Naming convention

Citizens are named `[Given] [Bole]-of-the-[cohort ordinal]`. A name states where you are
from and which Ember conditions you remember. Given names are short trade- and
craft-words; Bole names are the same, at scale.

## The circuit

Four licensed auditors. Each comes from a Bole that embodies one of the five positions
in `03-factions.md`, so that party arguments *are* faction arguments.

### Wend Cordwain-of-the-Eleventh — Cant-singer

Interfaces with the Strider; reads targets; writes and rewrites grafts. Inherited
maintenance duty over a set of ring passages she was taught as a girl in her
grandmother's kitchen and never questioned.

She is Act II Beat 8. She does not renounce her lineage, does not turn, and does not get
a redemption scene. She keeps performing the maintenance because the record degrades
without it, which means she is simultaneously the member most insistent on disclosure
and the one who must keep lying to preserve what would be disclosed.

### Pell Anneal-of-the-Tenth — Graft-medic

Tissue-splicing healer; places restorative grafts, excises hostile ones. From Anneal,
which makes him the party's resident Pruner sympathiser — not a plant or a traitor, just
someone raised on the arithmetic who finds the others sentimental.

He is the one who agrees with Verity most often and likes her least.

### Tarn Sill-of-the-Tenth — Coring

Heartwood quarrying; structural work; navigates by trace-dating. From Sill, which has
scheduled its own death. He left, which his family regards as apostasy, and he has never
resolved whether they were right.

Party navigator: the trace-dating traversal loop is his.

### Kite Marrow-of-the-Twelfth — Windreader

Storm and light forecasting; battlefield control and information. Youngest cohort — has
never experienced a fertile Ember cycle and has no patience for people who legislate as
though one is coming back.

From Marrow, so Act II's catastrophic Splice is her home and her fault by association.

## Verity Anneal-of-the-Ninth

Councilor, Pruner, and the arc's central pressure. Never fought. Never lies to the party.
Never wrong about arithmetic.

Appears in all three acts:

- **Act I** — dismantles the party's wrong case publicly and without malice, then admits
  privately she has seen the same smoothing in Anneal's rings and sat on it for six years
  because she did not want to know.
- **Act II** — moves to Prune Cordwain after the Marrow–Sill disaster. On the numbers,
  she is right.
- **Act III** — argues for Fusion. If the party chooses Bridge instead, she is the person
  who makes it logistically possible, while stating on the record that she believes it
  will kill more people than Fusion would have.

Design note: every impulse to give her a hypocrisy, a secret, or a comeuppance must be
refused. Her entire function is to be a decent person whose conclusion the player hates.
