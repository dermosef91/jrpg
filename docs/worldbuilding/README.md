# Second Harvest — Worldbuilding Bible

A JRPG setting about adapting to an indifferent natural fact.

The sun is in a variable dim phase. Nobody caused it, nobody can fix it, and the
cities are trees that walk. The player party are licensed auditors who read the
memory encoded in those trees' growth rings — and find that eleven generations ago,
someone edited it.

## The premise in one paragraph

Cities are built into **Striders**: city-scale migratory trees that creep across the
land chasing light at meters per year. A Strider's growth rings are a literal memory
medium, so history is a physical substance that can be read — and, by certain
inherited lineages, written. When the party finds a forty-ring stretch of record that
is chemically *too smooth* to be natural, in the same hand, in every city in the
world, they are not looking at fraud. They are looking at the founding of the world,
rewritten.
